Our story

Beauty, practiced properly

Beauty by Megan started with a simple idea: skin and nail care that's done properly, by people who actually trained for it, in a space that feels calm rather than clinical. No rushed appointments, no upsells you didn't ask for. Just skilled hands, honest advice, and treatments that hold up.

Years on, that hasn't changed. What started as one chair on the Overtoom has grown into a full studio, but the standard is the same: every treatment is done the way we'd want it done on ourselves.

Meet the founder

Megan

Megan founded Beauty by Megan on the idea that real results don't need needles. Trained in aesthetic osteopathy, she treats the face the way an osteopath treats the body: hands-on, structural, unhurried. She still takes clients herself, and every treatment plan in the studio starts with her eye.
