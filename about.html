The founder

Megan

Skin specialist & aesthetic osteopath

Megan didn't just create this beauty space. She built it to feel cozy, professional and comfortable for everyone who walks in. For her, beauty is a craft. Precise technique, genuine warmth and visible results, in every appointment, with every client. Her skincare treatments go beyond the basics. It's real attention to detail and care that's personal to you.

She'll find the right solution for your skin, guide you on how to treat it, and make sure you love what you see in the mirror. If your skin deserves the best, and it genuinely does, trust Megan. She knows exactly how to take care of it.
